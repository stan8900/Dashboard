### Deletion workflow

1. Authenticate the request and identify scope of personal data and affected systems.
2. Check applicable legal restrictions, disputes and valid preservation holds; log the decision and notify the requester as law requires.
3. Delete or irreversibly anonymise eligible data from active systems, including linked records and search indices.
4. Allow bounded backup expiry or implement restoration safeguards that prevent deleted data from being silently reintroduced.
5. Keep a minimal, access-restricted deletion audit record only for a documented necessary period.
